The main attraction

The Ultimate Taiwan
Wild Adventure Map

Thirteen secret spots around Taipei, plus twelve bonus hideaways across the island. Each one comes with the right tire for the road, and a Bridgestone pit stop on the way. It's basically a guide to the best spots for some public seggs. Scenic ones, obviously.

  1. 1

    The right tire for every route

    Tap a spot and we match the road. POTENZA RE-71RS for Yangmingshan hairpins, TURANZA 6 for the Tamsui sunset cruise, DUELER A/T002 for when the tarmac gives up.

  2. 2

    A pit stop on every route

    Every route passes an official Bridgestone tire center. Check the pressure, hydrate, compose yourselves.

  3. 3

    Stealth Mode

    If the system detects you're already en route, the spot will be temporarily hidden from others to avoid awkward encounters.
